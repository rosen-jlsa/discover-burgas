# Discover Burgas — product-led visual direction

## Goal

Use the clarity and product storytelling of a polished app launch to make the QR guide easy to understand before a visitor reaches a landmark. Preserve the cultural information, real map, QR routes, language support, nearby discovery, and external directions that make the product useful.

The Aevo reference informs layout and pacing only. Discover Burgas keeps its own name, writing, photography, screen content, and identity; no Aevo assets or copy are reused.

## Visual rules

- **Bright shell, strong type.** Use white and soft lavender surfaces, deep-violet typography, and a controlled violet–magenta–cyan spectrum inspired by a live music stage and the Black Sea. Keep long-form text readable outdoors.
- **Original event energy.** The palette may evoke the movement, light, and inclusivity of an international music event, but it must not reproduce official Eurovision logos or imply an official partnership.
- **Show the product immediately.** The centered landing hero pairs a short, direct promise with a real mobile-place preview, a live map action, and the no-install QR workflow.
- **Give every section a job.** Use generous space and alternating editorial/product demonstrations so map, verified stories, QR scanning, and next-place directions each read as a distinct capability.
- **Use actual content in the device.** The hero phone links to the published St. Anastasia page, map and route pages, and the existing local-storage favorite control.
- **Motion gives the page a rhythm.** The hero arrives in layers, sections reveal as visitors scroll, cards and controls respond to hover/press, and map markers gently animate into place. Reduced-motion settings remove nonessential movement without hiding content or interaction states.
- **Keep navigation and secondary pages consistent.** The compact dark capsule is limited to the header; public content pages retain light backgrounds and clear active states.
- **Do not claim an official partnership.** The original 2027 host-city link leads to the sourced Arena Burgas record. No Eurovision or Aevo marks are used.

## Homepage sequence

1. Centered promise, clear exploration actions, and a mobile-place preview.
2. A short QR-to-story-to-next-stop explanation.
3. Three visitor intents: culture, evening out, and accommodation.
4. The live interactive map and selected sourced landmark stories.
5. The keyboard-operable four-step phone walkthrough.
6. A practical central walking route with ordered stops.
7. Dining, accommodation, the sourced city story, and the jury demo CTA.

## Accessibility and motion

Controls remain semantic links and buttons; the hero favorite is the existing local-only favorite control. The phone walkthrough remains keyboard-operable. Visible focus styling is preserved. Motion is decorative and respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
